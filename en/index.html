Leads that close

From 2% to 7% close rate: a full calendar with leads at a third of the cost

  • Clínica Dr. Carrasco
  • Aesthetic surgery clinic
  • Meta Ads
Challenge
Leads were few and low quality.
Approach
Restructured acquisition channels on Meta and Google to prioritize lead quality, not just volume.
Results
Cost per lead dropped from ~CLP 3,000 to ~CLP 1,000. Close rate, from booking to paid surgery, went from 2% to 7%: 3.5 times more patients per booking.
Key learning
Cost per lead matters, but close rate is what pays the bills.

Leads that close

~150 sales meetings and ~6 franchises sold through Meta lead forms

  • Club Gordos · GRDS · Fast no Junk
  • Restaurant franchising
  • Meta Ads
Challenge
The target audience is tiny: people with tens of millions of pesos ready to invest.
Approach
Meta instant forms with qualifying questions: previous franchise experience and how much the person wanted to invest.
Results
Cost per lead of ~CLP 1,400 (GRDS), ~1,700 (Fast no Junk) and ~1,900 (Club Gordos). At least 3 sales meetings per week per brand for 4 months: around 150 in total.
Key learning
For high-ticket leads, qualifying in the form is worth more than a lower cost per lead: one pricier lead that books a meeting beats ten cheap ones.

Ecommerce sales

From a Cyber sale with zero revenue to CLP 17M the next one

  • Herramientas Express
  • Online hardware store
  • Meta Ads
Challenge
Sales were low: the 2022 Cyber event went by with practically no sales.
Approach
Optimized the product catalog by category, price and use case, so each product reached the people who need it.
Results
May 2023 Cyber event: ~CLP 17M in sales at ~8.5x ROAS. After Cyber, sales stabilized at CLP 8–10M a month, at 4–5x ROAS.
Key learning
In retail with large catalogs, the feed is the strategy.

Ecommerce sales

50x ROAS in fashion: ads that put the garment front and center

  • C. del Pilar
  • Fashion
  • Meta Ads
Challenge
The account was already efficient (22.5x ROAS) but small: the whole budget went to cold prospecting, remarketing was paused and the ads were generic brand creatives.
Approach
Replaced generic ads with ads built around specific garments: people see exactly what they can buy.
Results
ROAS went from 22.5x to 50x. Cost per purchase dropped from ~CLP 3,200 to ~CLP 1,800.
Key learning
In fashion, people buy a garment, not an abstract brand.

How I work

  1. 01

    Diagnosis

    I review accounts, tracking and the business before touching a campaign: what it sells, to whom and at what margin.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    Campaign structure, audiences and creative angles tied to a business goal, not to a single metric.

  3. 03

    Execution

    Meta and Google campaigns, catalogs, lead forms and automations so no lead goes cold.

  4. 04

    Full funnel

    I measure all the way to the sale: lead quality, close rate, repeat purchase. That's where money is made or lost.
