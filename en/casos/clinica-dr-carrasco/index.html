Context

An aesthetic surgery clinic that acquires patients through Meta Ads and Google Ads and books consultations through its own contact channels.

The problem

Leads were few and low quality. Ads were not converting and the booking process was leaking patients: only 2 out of every 100 people who booked ended up paying for surgery.

What I did

  • Restructured acquisition channels on Meta and Google to prioritize lead quality, not just volume.
  • Implemented the Meta Conversions API (CAPI) to feed the algorithm signals from real patients so it would optimize toward them.
  • Worked the full funnel with the clinic team: first contact, follow-up and booking.

Results

  • ✓Cost per lead dropped from ~CLP 3,000 to ~CLP 1,000.
  • ✓Close rate, from booking to paid surgery, went from 2% to 7%: 3.5 times more patients per booking.
  • ✓Weekly calendars filled up, averaging ~1,500 conversions a month between May and October 2026.

Approximate figures, rebuilt from each account’s reports. Amounts in Chilean pesos (CLP).

Takeaway

Cost per lead matters, but close rate is what pays the bills. Improving first contact and the signals the algorithm receives changed the business more than any bid tweak.
