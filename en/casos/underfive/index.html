Context

Underfive is a Chilean healthy snack brand made with five ingredients or fewer, sold in supermarkets and through its own online store. It spent about CLP 800K a month on Meta Ads.

The problem

The online store needed more new customers and bigger orders. Repeat purchase was the other big opportunity: at Underfive, the 4th order doubles customer lifetime value.

What I did

  • Dynamic catalog remarketing that shows each person the products they viewed.
  • A lead generation campaign to grow the email list at a low cost (~CLP 600 per lead).
  • A Klaviyo email plan with 8 customer segments based on purchase frequency and recency, plus flows that move each customer toward their next order.

Results

  • ✓9x account ROAS, with remarketing reaching 16.4x.
  • ✓Average order value rose to CLP 34,000.
  • ✓New customers grew by 40%.

Approximate figures, rebuilt from each account’s reports. Amounts in Chilean pesos (CLP).

Takeaway

Ads bring the customer in, but email brings them back. When both channels follow the same segmentation logic, new customers and order value grow at the same time.
