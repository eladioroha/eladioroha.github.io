Context

Underfive is a Chilean healthy snack brand made with five ingredients or fewer, sold in supermarkets and through its own online store.

The problem

Repeat purchase was the big opportunity: the 4th order doubles customer lifetime value.

What I did

  • Dynamic catalog remarketing that shows each person the products they viewed.
  • A lead generation campaign to grow the email list at a low cost.
  • A Klaviyo email plan with 8 customer segments based on purchase frequency and recency, plus flows that move each customer toward their next order.

Results

  • ✓Remarketing at 16.4x ROAS.
  • ✓Leads at ~CLP 600 each.
