Context

Herramientas Express is an online hardware store selling through Meta Ads and Google Ads.

The problem

Sales were low: the 2022 Cyber event went by with practically no sales. Products looked the same as the competition and the ads were not speaking to anyone in particular.

What I did

  • Optimized the product catalog by category, price and use case, so each product reached the people who need it.
  • Reworked ads and creatives to target the ideal customer.
  • Restructured campaigns and bidding on Meta and Google.

Results

  • ✓May 2023 Cyber event: ~CLP 17M in sales at ~8.5x ROAS.
  • ✓After Cyber, sales stabilized at CLP 8–10M a month, at 4–5x ROAS.

Approximate figures, rebuilt from each account’s reports. Amounts in Chilean pesos (CLP).

Takeaway

In retail with large catalogs, the feed is the strategy. Organizing the catalog around how people actually shop paid off more than any single ad.
